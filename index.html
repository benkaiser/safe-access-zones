Why this matters

Understanding safe-access boundaries helps pro-life advocates conduct peaceful, lawful advocacy while respecting the people who use and work at these services.

Every human life matters. Informed public advocacy and policy engagement are important ways to take a stand on abortion policy and support alternatives for women and families.
